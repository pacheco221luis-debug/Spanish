A LITTLE EVERYDAY SPANISH

Words you can use out there.

Small phrases can make everyday moments easier — ordering lunch, finding your way or meeting someone new.

  • Ordering Food

    “Me regala dos pupusas de queso, por favor.” Two cheese pupusas, please. ("Me regala" is the local friendly way to order). Order food, coffee, and drinks with confidence at local spots.

  • Shopping at Markets

    “¿Cuánto cuesta?” How much does it cost? Ask prices and chat naturally with vendors while shopping.

  • Asking for Directions

    “¿Dónde queda…?” Where is…? Ask for directions and understand local landmarks.

  • Meeting Local People

    “Mucho gusto.” Nice to meet you. Break the ice and introduce yourself smoothly.

  • Everyday Expressions

    “¡Qué chivo!” How cool! (Salvadoran slang) Understand informal expressions and when to use them.

  • Getting Around

    “¿A qué hora sale el bus?” What time does the bus leave? Handle transport and travel details with simple phrases.
