Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Version 1.1

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Spanish with Lisa (“we”, “our” or “us”), operated by Lisa in Santa Ana, El Salvador, is responsible for the personal data you submit through this website. For any question about your data, send a message through the inquiry form on this website or tell Lisa directly in your conversation with her.

2. Information we collect

When you send an inquiry through the form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number / WhatsApp contact
  • Email address
  • Your Spanish level and learning interests
  • Preferred days and time range for lessons
  • Optional comments or goals

We also record the time of your consent, the version of this policy you agreed to, and a security token that Cloudflare Turnstile uses to check that the form is being sent by a person and not a bot.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

We use your information only to contact you, discuss your goals and arrange personalised one-on-one Spanish lessons. We process it based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, where applicable), which you give by ticking the consent box. Sending the form does not book or confirm a lesson.

4. Where your information is stored and who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts this website, delivers its content and provides Turnstile, the bot-protection check on the form.
  • Google Sheets stores the inquiries you send in a private spreadsheet that only authorised administrators can access.
  • Google Fonts supplies this site’s typefaces. When a page loads, your browser may contact Google’s servers, which can receive your IP address and browser details.

5. International transfers

Lisa is based in El Salvador, and the providers above may process data on servers in other countries, including the United States. We rely on the safeguards these providers offer for international transfers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and related correspondence for up to 12 months without active lesson engagement. After that, personal records are deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your personal data, and you can withdraw your consent for future communications whenever you wish. Send your request through the inquiry form on this website or tell Lisa directly. If the GDPR applies to you, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and its version number. The version you agreed to is recorded with your inquiry.
